Forecast and drift

The placement plans look at a demand forecast for each zone and hour, made seven days ahead. A monitor checks every week whether the forecast error has grown or the data has changed.

Weekly forecast error (WAPE), October 2024 to January 2025

WAPE is the total absolute forecast miss divided by the total number of requests. Lower is better.

How it works

From raw trip files to the hypothesis tests, in the order the project was built.

The six placement plans

Money rules

revenue = $ + $ x passenger miles
costs = tolls + $ x all miles + $ per idle car-hour
profit = revenue - costs

All miles include the empty miles a car drives to a pickup or to a new waiting spot. Tips are never counted. The price is a calibration to recorded fares, not a pricing proposal. Riders are assumed not to react to it.

Simulator rules in brief

  • The real hourly request counts are thinned to 1% and split into four 15-minute slots.
  • A request is served by an idle car in its own zone, else the nearest idle car in a neighbouring zone, else it is lost.
  • Trips that end outside the launch area are dropped (a geofence) and count as lost.
  • Each day starts fresh. Plans can move only idle cars, at the start of each hour; moves cost empty miles.
  • Every setting was frozen on October days before any test day was run.
